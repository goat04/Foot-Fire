The Origin

Our Story

Hi, I am Sri Manvi Karangula, a Class 9 student from Sherwood Public School, Secunderabad.

Growing up playing football on hard dirt grounds, I noticed a major problem: standard football shoes wear out too quickly under extreme friction, and high-end brands cost too much for everyday players. That's why I founded Foot Fire.

We are on a mission to bridge the gap—delivering tough, durable, and affordable football footwear forged specifically for the harsh realities of local dirt pitches, empowering every passionate player to chase their game without boundaries.
